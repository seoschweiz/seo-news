The street artist who transforms trash into art In the world of street art, where creative boundaries exist, few artists challenge conventions as radically as Art Is Trash. Behind this provocative name is Francisco de Pájaro, a Spanish street artist known for his unique approach: transforming discarded objects, trash, and old furniture into spontaneous and profound works of art. Instead of painting on walls or canvases, Art Is Trash uses what society throws away, breathing new life into the seemingly worthless.
